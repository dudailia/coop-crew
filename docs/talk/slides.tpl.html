It finds the roles, researches the companies and prepares every application overnight. Nothing is submitted or sent until you approve that exact item.

4
the review page

One page, every morning

Questions for you (HOLD) when a role might break a lock
Review packs brief, resume, answers, soft flags, field log
Approve or Skip by id, nothing else moves
5
safety, in code

Nothing leaves without you

prepare mode

Forms are filled, emails drafted. Sending mode is a setting you change yourself.

approval of that exact id

/approve id or APPROVE.txt. A ready pack is not permission.

kill switch

/stop halts the cycle at its next step; every acting tool call is blocked until /crew-resume.

guard hook

One door to submit, one to send. Agents cannot approve, edit the pipeline or change the safety files.

6
nuworks, within the rules

Your email and your files, not a bot

On by default

  • NUworks job-alert emails from your saved searches
  • Postings you save as PDF or HTML into data/nuworks-import/
  • You always apply in NUworks yourself

Browser reader: off

  • Read-only, kept in the repo, never on by default
  • Turns on only after a terms notice and a typed acknowledgement

Symplicity's terms bar "automated software, tools, devices, agents, scripts" used to navigate or access the site without permission.

7
the resume and the checker

No invented facts

One page, ATS-plain the build fails at two
Every number and name must be in your facts.md or the posting
"40%" and "Google" fail when you never wrote them; "40+" and your real employer pass
Work authorization your sentence, word for word
