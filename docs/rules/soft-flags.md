# Soft flags

A soft flag is something the student should see before approving, that does not block the pack. Write them in
`out/<id>/soft-flags.md`, one line each:

- The posting's dates differ from the co-op dates by up to two weeks.
- Pay is not posted, or posted below the pay floor only as a range that overlaps it.
- Hybrid with more office days than the student prefers but within their lock.
- The posting is older than 30 days, or its deadline is within 3 days.
- A required question was answered from a standing answer (form-answers skill) rather than from the student.
- The employer is new to the student's list and the three-step check (self-developed mode) found an incidental word.

Hard conflicts are not soft flags: a lock conflict is a HOLD with the verbatim question, and a failed eligibility rule
is DQ.
