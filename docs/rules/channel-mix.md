# Channel mix: how each KEEP role is pursued

| Role | Channel | Who acts |
|---|---|---|
| Posting with an online form (company board) | `form`: tailored packet, JEV prepares the form, `/approve` submits | applicant, after approval |
| NUworks posting | `nuworks`: tailored packet, "Apply in NUworks" | the student, in NUworks |
| Posting that says "email your resume to ..." | `email`: an email with the resume, planned in a send window | outreach, after approval |
| Strong-fit company with no posting | `email` to a published address, or a LinkedIn note to a verified person | outreach (email) or the student (LinkedIn, by hand) |

One named person per company at most, and one email per person ever.
