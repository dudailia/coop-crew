# Banned employers (self-developed co-op mode)

A self-developed co-op cannot be with an employer that already recruits through NUworks. In self-developed mode every
employer is checked against this list (employer-wide, any spelling) and through the three-step check in the
banned-check skill. Lines the student unbanned stay with a note: `- Name [UNBANNED 2026-11-12: no Northeastern link]`.
One per line as `- Name` or `- Name | evidence URL`.
